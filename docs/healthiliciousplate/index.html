🔒 Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Healthilicious Plate — Last updated: September 9, 2026

Healthilicious Plate ("we", "us", "our") operates the healthiliciousplate.com website and the Healthilicious Plate Android app (package name com.healthiliciousplate.app). This page explains what information we collect through both, why we collect it, and how it's used. Most of the app works with no personal information at all — no account or login is required.

Information we collect

Tools you use without giving us any information

The BMI Calculator and the Dosha Quiz process the numbers or answers you enter entirely to produce your result and are not saved against your identity in any way — we don't ask for your name, email, or any account to use them.

Diet Plan Generator

We ask for your age, weight, height, gender, goal, activity level, food preference, and any health condition you select, so we can calculate a personalized 7-day plan. If you choose to enter your email address to receive a copy, we store that email address along with the plan summary so we can follow up with related nutrition tips. Entering an email is optional — the plan is generated and shown to you either way.

Make It Healthy (ingredient photo tool)

If you use this tool, the photo you take or choose is sent securely to a third-party AI vision service (NVIDIA NIM) to identify ingredients and suggest a healthy recipe. The photo passes through our server only to forward it to that service — we do not save a copy of it. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo for this tool; the app works fully without it if you'd rather pick an existing image or skip the tool entirely.

Contact, consultation, and newsletter forms

If you book a consultation, send us a message, or sign up for updates, we collect what you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, and the message or concern you share — so we can respond to you or send the updates you asked for.

Ebook purchases

Buying an ebook requires your name, email, and payment reference so we can verify your order and deliver the ebook. Payment itself is handled directly between you and our payment provider (UPI / Google Pay) — we do not receive or store your card, bank, or UPI PIN details.

Automatically collected technical data

Like most websites and apps, our server logs standard technical information (such as IP address and request timing) for security and to prevent abuse of our free tools — for example, to apply fair-use limits on how often the AI-powered tools can be used from one connection. We don't use this to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To provide the tool, plan, or content you asked for
  • To respond to consultation requests, questions, and orders
  • To send nutrition tips or updates you've opted into (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To keep our free tools available to everyone by preventing abuse

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not run third-party advertising in the mobile app.

Third-party services we use

The Diet Plan Generator uses OpenRouter to generate plan text, and Make It Healthy uses NVIDIA NIM to analyze food photos — both receive only the specific input needed to perform that one request (your stated goals, or the photo), not your identity. Consultation bookings are handled via Cal.com. Ebook checkout uses your device's UPI / Google Pay app directly. Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data that passes through them.

Data retention and deletion

We keep contact, consultation, order, and newsletter records for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business records. If you'd like your information deleted, email us at hello@healthiliciousplate.com and we'll remove it, except where we're required to keep records (for example, completed order records) for legal or accounting reasons.

Children's privacy

Healthilicious Plate is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and website evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@healthiliciousplate.com.
