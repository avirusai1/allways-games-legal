Privacy Policy — Metro India News

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This policy applies to the Metro India News website (metroindianews.com) and the Metro India News Android app (com.metroindianews.app). It explains what information is collected, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • The app stores a randomly generated, anonymous device identifier so likes and saved stories work. It is not linked to your identity.
  • If you post a comment, the display name you type, your comment text, and your IP address are stored.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or files.
  • The Android app shows ads. Google's ad network uses your device's advertising ID to select and measure them; you can reset or delete that ID in Android settings at any time.

Information we collect

1. Anonymous device identifier (app)

The first time you open the app, it generates a random UUID and stores it on your device. It is sent with requests so that likes, saved stories, and share counts are remembered for your device, and it is stored on our servers only in hashed form. It is generated by the app itself — it is not your Android ID, advertising ID, IMEI, or any other hardware identifier, and it cannot be used to identify you personally. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage erases it permanently; a fresh, unrelated identifier is generated on next launch.

2. Comments

If you choose to submit a comment, we store the display name you enter, the comment text, the article it belongs to, and the IP address the comment was sent from. The IP address is retained for spam prevention and abuse moderation. Comments are reviewed before they are published. Anything you write in a comment becomes publicly visible once approved, so please do not include personal information there.

3. Technical request data

Like any website or app backend, our servers automatically record standard request information — IP address, date and time, requested page, and user agent — in server logs used for security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing faults.

4. Preferences stored on your device

Your theme choice (light/dark), followed topics, reading history, and saved-story list are stored locally on your device or browser and are never sent to our servers. On the website, a cookie holding a random visitor token is used for the like/bookmark features when no device identifier is available.

Advertising

Metro India News is free to read and is paid for by advertising.

In the Android app, ads are served by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google receives your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for this purpose), your IP address, and coarse information about your device. This identifier is controlled by you: Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices lets you reset it, or delete it entirely so that no personalised ads can be served. Ads inside the app are always labelled “Sponsored”.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised advertising is served, using Google's consent interface. You can withdraw or change that choice at any time.

Google's handling of this data is described at business.safety.google/privacy.

The website

The website displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads; you can review and control this at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and adssettings.google.com.

Article images may be loaded from third-party image hosts and content delivery networks. Those providers receive your IP address as a normal part of delivering the image.

How we use information

  • To show you news content and remember what you liked or saved
  • To count aggregate views, likes, and shares on articles
  • To moderate comments and prevent spam and abuse
  • To keep the service secure, available, and working correctly

We do not use your information for profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent it.

Sharing

We share information only with the infrastructure providers that run this service on our behalf (server hosting and content delivery), with Google (AdMob in the app, AdSense on the website) as described above, and where we are required to do so by law or to protect our rights.

Retention

Interaction records tied to a hashed device identifier are kept while the content remains published. Comments are kept until they are deleted by you (on request) or by our moderators. Server logs are retained for a short period for security and diagnostics.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset your identifier: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app; on the website, clear cookies and site data.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Comment removal: email us with the article and comment details and we will remove it.
  • Access or deletion: email us and we will act on your request. Because we do not hold account details, please include the information needed to locate the relevant records.

Children

The service is a general-audience news product and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a comment, contact us and we will remove it. Advertising in the app is not targeted at children.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or files
  • We do not sell your data

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS, and device identifiers are stored hashed rather than in raw form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.
