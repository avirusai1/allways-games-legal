Privacy Policy — Sirat

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Sirat is an Islamic companion app for prayer times, Qur'an reading, adhkar, recitation, and learning. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your data. If a claim here does not match the app's actual behaviour, the app's behaviour is wrong and should be reported — see the contact section below.

The short version: Sirat is built offline-first. Your prayer logs, Qur'an reading progress, bookmarks, habit tracking, and reflection journal are stored only on your device. We do not run any analytics or advertising SDK, we do not have user accounts, and we do not see or sell your worship data. The only things that leave your device are: (1) requests to a Qur'an-recitation content delivery network when you stream or download audio, and (2) purchase information handled entirely by Google Play when you subscribe.

1. Information we do not collect

Sirat has no sign-in system. There are no user accounts, no email collection, and no server that Sirat operates which receives your personal or worship data. The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored locally, in the app's private storage, and is never transmitted anywhere by Sirat:

  • Prayer logs and prayer-tracking history
  • Qur'an reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights
  • Habit-tracking entries and streaks
  • Your private reflection journal entries
  • AI companion conversation history (see §5)
  • App preferences: chosen calculation method, madhhab, reciter, language, theme, and your saved or detected location

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device. Sirat does not currently offer cloud backup or cross-device sync; if that changes in a future version, it will be clearly opt-in and this policy will be updated first.

3. Location

Sirat asks for location permission (coarse or fine) for one purpose only: calculating accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for where you are. That calculation happens entirely on your device. Your coordinates are stored locally as part of your app preferences and are never sent to any server.

You do not have to grant location permission to use the app — you can instead search for your city from an offline database bundled with the app, with no permission required at all.

4. Qur'an recitation audio

When you stream or download a reciter's audio, your device requests the specific verse file from the Islamic Network content delivery network (cdn.islamic.network). That request necessarily includes your device's IP address, as with any network request, but Sirat does not attach any personal identifier to it and does not log or store this traffic itself. Downloaded recitation files are cached on your device for offline playback.

5. The AI companion

Sirat's AI companion ("Ask Sirat") answers from a knowledge base bundled with the app — the Qur'an, translations, hadith, and duas already stored on your device. In the current version, this runs entirely on-device: your questions are not sent to any external AI service or server. Conversation history is stored locally so you can revisit past answers, and you can delete this history at any time from within the app.

If a future version of Sirat introduces a server-connected AI service, this policy will be updated in advance to disclose exactly what is sent, and that change will not be applied silently.

6. Subscriptions and payment

Sirat offers an optional paid subscription (Premium or Family) after a 30-day free trial. All payment is handled by Google Play Billing. Sirat never receives or stores your card details, billing address, or any other payment information — that is handled entirely by Google, under Google's own privacy policy. Sirat only receives confirmation of your subscription status (active, trial, expired) from Google Play, stored locally on your device.

8. Data safety and your rights

Because your worship data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes that data. There is no server-side account to close and no server-side copy to request or delete, because none exists. If you have used the AI companion's history or the reflection journal and want to clear them without uninstalling, both can be cleared from within the app.

9. Children

Sirat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app itself contains no mechanism for a user to submit personal information to us in the first place.

10. Changes to this policy

If Sirat's data practices change — for example, if cloud sync or a server-connected AI service is introduced — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above, before that change ships in the app.
