Privacy Policy — Allways Games

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This policy explains what Allways Games (“the app”) does with data. It is written to be accurate rather than reassuring: where data is collected, it says so.

The short version

The app has no accounts, no login, and no server of its own. Your puzzle progress lives only on your device. The app does, however, show ads and support an in-app purchase, and both of those involve Google collecting data. That is described below.

What stays on your device

  • Which puzzles you have completed, and on what day
  • Your streaks and per-game statistics
  • Your settings

This is stored locally on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere, it is not readable by us, and deleting the app deletes it. There is no backup and no way to recover it or move it to another device.

What Google collects

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version)
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)

We never receive this data ourselves; we only see aggregate counts of ads shown. Google's handling of it is governed by their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Privacy → Ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we never see your card number, billing address, or any other payment information. We receive only a token confirming that a purchase exists, which the app uses to switch ads off.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ads shown in the app, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • We do not require an account
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites ourselves
  • We do not sell data, because we do not hold any

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is used only to load ads and to reach Google Play for purchases. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
